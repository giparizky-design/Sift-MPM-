## 1. Executive Summary

Following the root-cause discovery of data leakage and artificial mine-halo cheating in Audit v2.6 ([`audits/2026-10-31_v2.6_kutai_basin_cheating_and_leakage_audit.md`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/audits/2026-10-31_v2.6_kutai_basin_cheating_and_leakage_audit.md)), this audit documents the deep adversarial review, fix implementation, and verification of all 4 **Gemini Argon** architectural enhancements alongside the spatial cross-validation negative truncation remediation.

Skeptical adversarial review identified 4 critical vulnerabilities in the initial implementation:
1. **Model Introspection Channel Mismatch:** `model_introspection.py` loaded `cube_raw = stack.array` without filtering out banned non-feature columns (such as `geology_coal_contact_dist_km`), causing shape broadcast mismatches and PyTorch channel mismatch crashes against trained U-Net checkpoints.
2. **Streaming Inference Distance Skew:** `execute_full_raster_chunked_inference` in `scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py` did not cap incoming distance features in streaming parquet batches, evaluating trees on unclipped distances while trees were trained on clipped distances.
3. **U-Net Input Distance GPS Grid Smuggling:** `cube_norm` channels in `scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py` were not capped at the 15.0 km threshold, leaving long-range coordinates in the U-Net input for neural spatial encoding.
4. **Missing `dist_sedimentary_polygon_km` in Core Features:** `compute_lithologic_and_stratigraphic_contacts` in `src/features/coal_and_multimodal_features.py` omitted `dist_sedimentary_polygon_km` from its returned dictionary.

All 4 vulnerabilities have been remediated, verified, and protected with dedicated unit tests. Every vector of target and filter leakage has been eliminated while strictly preserving the stability, performance, and backward compatibility of the metallic SIFT $K=4$ prospectivity engine (*porphyry Cu*, *skarn Fe-Cu*, *epithermal Au*, *laterite Fe-Ni*).

All **171 unit tests** pass with zero failures and zero warnings.

## 3. Stability of Metallic SIFT K=4 Domain

A critical constraint of this remediation was ensuring that the stable metallic model domain (SIFT $K=4$) remained completely undisturbed:
1. **Zero Model Regressions:** `TwoStageK4Ensemble` continues to execute with identical Bagged XGBoost heads, Elkan-Noto priors, and OutMOGC closed boundary envelopes.
2. **Quarantine Isolation:** Quarantining coal-bearing formation binary masks and coal concession distances in `NON_FEATURE_COLS` and `_FORBIDDEN_RAW_BAND_PATTERNS` actively prevents cross-domain contamination of metallic heads (such as the Skarn Fe-Cu leak noted in Audit v2.6 §1.6).
3. **Igneous Seed Purity:** Purging `"Tembaga"` (copper) from intrusive seeds guarantees that porphyry and skarn copper heads are guided by authentic plutonic geology rather than their own target labels.
4. **Introspection Channel Safety:** Aligning `cube_raw` channels to `feature_cols` guarantees that metallic target activation extraction in `model_introspection.py` never fails on multi-layer stacks.
