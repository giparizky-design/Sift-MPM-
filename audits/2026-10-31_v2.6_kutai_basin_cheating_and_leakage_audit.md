## 1. Executive Summary

A comprehensive, line-by-line code and empirical tensor audit confirms that the model's visual precision in pinpointing actual mines in the SIFT Exploration Workspace is **not an authentic machine learning discovery of subsurface coal geology**. It is the direct consequence of **6 compounding leakage channels**:

1. **Target Distance Masquerade:** The feature named [`geology_coal_contact_dist_km`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/harvest_kalimantan_multimodal.py#L506-L520) is **not** a stratigraphic formation contact distance. It is literally the **exact Euclidean distance transform to the 428 ground-truth coal mines/concessions** in [`data/borneo/kalimantan_coal_deposits.csv`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/data/borneo/kalimantan_coal_deposits.csv).
2. **Label Filter Leakage:** The binary layer [`in_coal_bearing_formation`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/build_kutai_basin_100m.py#L48) was quarantined in the HD builder but **accidentally omitted from `NON_FEATURE_COLS` in [`scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py:556-571`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py#L556-L571)**. Because the label generator strictly rejected 100% of candidates outside this formation mask, XGBoost seized upon it with **43.6% to 50.4% feature importance**, using it as the root split in **61% to 100% of all decision trees**.
3. **Cross-Validation Bypass:** Spatial cross-validation (10 km blocks) was completely bypassed because `geology_coal_contact_dist_km` was precomputed on the full island *before* fold assignment. Held-out validation cells were evaluated using their distance to known mines inside that same validation block.
4. **Validation Negative Truncation Bug:** In [`scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py:1927`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py#L1927), the spatial CV evaluation capped validation negatives to **strictly 600 cells**, while retaining up to **3,541 positive cells**. This artificially inflated validation prevalence to **81.03%**, resulting in the illusion of an out-of-fold PR-AUC of **0.9953** in [`spatial_cv_metrics_coal.csv`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/outputs/seven_pass_kutai_basin_100m/spatial_cv_metrics_coal.csv).
5. **DAE U-Net Sparsity Corruption:** The DAE U-Net did not fail (PR-AUC 0.075) because convolutions cannot learn 1D step-functions; it failed because [`scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py:808-820`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py#L808-L820) constructed the 2D input cube `cube_norm` by scattering an 80,000-cell subsampled tabular dataframe across an island grid, leaving **>98% of the raster cells as empty zeros**. The resulting low U-Net score forced the Bayesian ensemble to assign 70% weight to XGBoost, allowing the leaked tabular distance tree splits to dominate the final GeoTIFF.
6. **Cross-Domain Metallic Contamination:** In [`outputs/seven_pass_kalimantan_50m/targets/site_cards.json:96-98`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/outputs/seven_pass_kalimantan_50m/targets/site_cards.json#L96-L98), metallic models (Skarn Fe-Cu) also ingested `geology_coal_contact_dist_km` with a large positive SHAP attribution (`+0.5538`). Furthermore, in [`src/features/coal_and_multimodal_features.py:568`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/src/features/coal_and_multimodal_features.py#L568), `dist_calc_alkaline_intrusive_km` explicitly used GeoRIMA copper deposits (`commodity_label == "Tembaga"`) as intrusive seeds, contaminating metallic porphyry/skarn heads with coordinate proximity leakage as well.
