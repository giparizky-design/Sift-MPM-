# v4.5 — Deposit-Level Buffered Spatial CV Baseline (East Kalimantan, 250 m)

**Date:** 2026-11-09 · **Timestamp:** 2026-11-09T15:00:00+08:00 · **Type:** Evaluation / training audit
**Code:** [`src/evaluation/deposit_cv.py`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/src/evaluation/deposit_cv.py), [`scripts/run_deposit_cv.py`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/scripts/run_deposit_cv.py) (CHANGELOG v4.5.0)
**Outputs:** [`outputs/deposit_cv/`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/outputs/deposit_cv/)

## Why
The pipeline's reported metrics are pixel PR-AUC from `run_seven_pass_training.py`. They have four problems:
- The pixel metrics come from a 30-tree, 2-bag XGBoost refit inside unbuffered folds (L2211-L2245), not from the production model.
- The production model's fusion weights and ĉ come from a single validation fold.
- `blind_holdout.json` is a copy of the OOF metrics (L2275, L2469), not a held-out region.
- Each deposit point is rasterized as a 1 km disk (about 50 cells), so pixel metrics count one deposit 50 times.

This audit sets an honest baseline measured at the level of the deposit.

## Method
1. **Deposits.** 8-connected clusters of positive cells. The metal heads collapse to very few independent deposits:

   | Head | Positive cells | Deposits |
   |---|---|---|
   | porphyry_cu | 556 | 10 |
   | skarn_fe_cu | 719 | 13 |
   | epithermal_au | 616 | 11 |
   | laterite_fe_ni | 55 | 1 |
   | any metal | — | 33 (heads overlap) |
   | coal (K=3 heads) | — | 361 |

2. **Folds.** Deposits are grouped by the 25 km block holding their centroid, and whole blocks are assigned to 5 balanced folds.
3. **Buffer.** Held-out deposits and every cell within the buffer (5 km by default; 15 km for the sensitivity run) are removed from training.
4. **Preprocessing.** All production preprocessing is refit on training cells only: feature selection, 15 km distance caps, logistics propensity, K=4 exclusion masks, asymmetric head weights, OUTMOGC gates and Elkan-Noto scaling. Training uses all remaining positives plus 80k background cells, as production does. Every fold trains from zero: 100 trees, 5 bags, depth 6 (metals) or 5 (coal), learning rate 0.05.
5. **Score.** A deposit's score is the maximum score over its cells. Its **area fraction** is the share of 500k randomly sampled background cells that score higher; ties count half.
6. **Success-rate AUC.** 1 − mean(area fraction). 0.5 is a random map. Reported with a bootstrap 95% CI and a Monte Carlo p-value against a random map. Heads with fewer than 5 deposits are flagged `insufficient_data`.
7. **Baselines.** L2 logistic regression, and the best single feature (feature and sign chosen on training deposits only).

**Not reproduced:** the DAE U-Net and its PR-AUC blend (66% of the coal weight in production), the validation-fold ĉ, and warm-starting.

## Findings
1. **The models rank held-out deposits far above chance; pixel PR-AUC understated them.** The old OOF metrics were metals PR-AUC 0.078 and coal 0.033. At deposit level, metals XGBoost places half of the held-out deposits in the top 4% of the map (AUC 0.90), and coal reaches AUC 0.83. Every result except laterite has p < 0.001 against a random map.
2. **XGBoost adds little over simple baselines.**
   - Metals: logistic regression scores 0.86, and `dilation_tendency_Td` alone scores 0.86, against XGBoost's 0.90. The confidence intervals overlap with 33 deposits.
   - Coal: logistic regression is within 0.015 of XGBoost.
3. **One GEM-derived fault channel carries most of the metal signal.** That is consistent with the v4.4 gain-share finding, which put 59–77% of gain on fault-derived channels. Whether it reflects geology, or the GEM fault traces being mapped more densely around known mines, cannot be decided from these data. It needs a check against an independent fault or lineament source.
4. **SAR adds nothing for metals on this test.** The 21-feature schema without SAR scores 0.910, against 0.899 with SAR; the difference is within noise. The 27-feature runs were also much slower per fold (100–524 s against 22–30 s). The wall times are noisy because other jobs ran in parallel, but there is a consistent gap worth profiling.
5. **Some of the 5 km result is spatial proximity.** Widening the buffer to 15 km lowers metals XGBoost from 0.899 to 0.857. That is still strong, but the 15 km figure is the more conservative one to quote.
6. **Calibration hurts ranking for epithermal_au.** The OUTMOGC gate and Elkan-Noto scaling lower its AUC from 0.845 to 0.766, which suggests the gate's GMM envelope, fitted on about 9 training deposits, is too narrow. Porphyry and skarn change little.
7. **Coal is broad but not sharp.** AUC is 0.83, but only 2–3% of coal deposits fall in the top 1% of area and the median deposit sits at 13% of the area. The model separates basin from non-basin and does not pinpoint deposits. That fits the missing stratigraphy (formation) input.
8. **Laterite cannot be evaluated or learned from the labels.** It has 1 deposit and is flagged `insufficient_data`.

## Caveats
- With 10–13 deposits per metal head, per-head CIs are wide. Treat the head ranking as indicative only.
- **Background sample.** The background is a fixed random sample of 500k cells. Its standard error on an area fraction is below 0.001.
- **Scoring rule.** Max-over-cluster scoring is lenient for large clusters. The median-cell variant is in `deposit_cv_summary.json` (`success_rate_auc_median_cell`).
- **Mirrored, not imported.** The runner copies the orchestrator's feature-exclusion set and access columns instead of importing them. If the orchestrator changes, the two can drift apart.
- **Leak found in the orchestrator's own OOF loop (not fixed).** `run_seven_pass_training.py` reuses `exclusion_masks` and `sample_weights_k4` built from the production fold's training positives inside every OOF fold. Held-out deposits in other folds therefore still shape which unlabeled cells count as negatives. This harness recomputes them per fold.

## Recommendations
1. Use this harness, at a 15 km buffer, as the acceptance test for every model change. Quote success-rate AUC and capture at 5% and 10%, not pixel PR-AUC.
2. Run a drop-column study on the fault-derived channels (`dilation_tendency_Td`, `slip_tendency_Ts`, `dist_active_faults_m`, `active_fault_density`). Check them against an independent lineament source.
3. Evaluate whether the U-Net blend helps, by adding a DAE adapter, before it keeps a production weight.
4. Merge the magmatic-hydrothermal heads or pool analogue arcs. Retire the laterite head, or replace it with a knowledge-driven model.
5. Revisit the OUTMOGC gate for heads with fewer than about 15 training deposits.
6. Add a stratigraphic (formation) input for coal.
