## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Directive

This audit certifies the completion of the **Autonomous Kalimantan Multimodal Geospatial Data Ingestion Mission** for the SIFT Mineral Prospectivity Mapping (MPM) platform. The ingestion pipeline services both the **Borneo $K=4$ Metallic Discovery Engine** (Porphyry Cu, Skarn Fe-Cu, Epithermal Au, Laterite Ni) and the **Coal Prospectivity Engine** across the entire island of Borneo/Kalimantan.

### Spatial Domain & Grid Definitions
- **Geographic Bounds:** `[108.5° E, -4.5° S, 119.5° E, 4.5° N]` (WGS84, `EPSG:4326`).
- **Spatial Span:** $11.0^\circ$ longitude $\times 9.0^\circ$ latitude ($\approx 1,225\text{ km} \times 1,000\text{ km}$).
- **Regional Island-Scale Resolution:** Isotropic $0.0025^\circ$ ($\approx 250\text{ m}$ at equator / Kalimantan).
  - **Dimensions:** $H = 3,600\text{ rows} \times W = 4,400\text{ columns} = 15,840,000\text{ cells}$.
  - **Affine Transform:** `Affine(0.0025, 0.0, 108.5, 0.0, -0.0025, 4.5)`.
  - **Valid Onshore Land Cells:** $8,247,719$ cells ($52.1\%$ of the bounding box).
- **Hotspot Mining Belt Scale:** High-resolution $50\text{ m}$ grids ($0.00045^\circ$, $2,000 \times 2,000\text{ px}$):
  1. *Mahakam Delta Hotspot* (`[116.8° E, -1.0° S, 117.8° E, 0.0° N]`).
  2. *Barito Foreland Hotspot* (`[114.8° E, -2.8° S, 115.8° E, -1.8° S]`).
  3. *Meratus Suture Hotspot* (`[115.0° E, -3.8° S, 116.0° E, -2.8° S]`).

## 2. Seven Modular Ingestion Workstreams

The autonomous pipeline ([`scripts/harvest_kalimantan_multimodal.py`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/scripts/harvest_kalimantan_multimodal.py)) executes seven modular harvesting and feature derivation workstreams:

### Workstream 1: Synthetic Aperture Radar (ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 L-Band)
- **Sensor:** JAXA ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 L-Band SAR ($\lambda \approx 23.6\text{ cm}$).
- **Bands Harvested:** High-resolution orthorectified $HH$ and $HV$ backscatter ($\gamma^0$).
- **Physics Derivatives:**
  - Depolarization Ratio: $\text{DPR} = \gamma^0_{HV} / (\gamma^0_{HH} + \epsilon)$.
  - Structural Lineament Filtering: Steger directional ridge lineament filter ($4\times$ angles $0^\circ, 45^\circ, 90^\circ, 135^\circ$, $\sigma=1.5$) extracting deep structural fracture lineaments through tropical regolith.
- **Interim Artifacts:** `data/kalimantan/interim/palsar2_hh_25m.tif`, `palsar2_hv_25m.tif`, `palsar2_dpr_25m.tif`.
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `sar_palsar2_hh.tif`, `sar_palsar2_hv.tif`, `sar_palsar2_dpr.tif`, `sar_palsar2_lineament_density.tif`.

### Workstream 2: Optical Multispectral (Sentinel-2 L2A Dry-Season Composite)
- **Sensor:** ESA Sentinel-2 MSI (Level-2A Bottom-Of-Atmosphere reflectance).
- **Harvest Window:** Dry-season window (August–September) with SCL scene classification cloud/shadow masking.
- **Physics Indices:**
  - $\text{NDVI} = (B8 - B4) / (B8 + B4)$
  - $\text{NDRE} = (B8 - B5) / (B8 + B5)$
  - Albedo $= 0.356 \cdot B2 + 0.130 \cdot B4 + 0.373 \cdot B8 + 0.085 \cdot B11 + 0.072 \cdot B12$
  - Wood/Cellulose Debris Index: $\text{WCDI} = (B11 - B12) / (B11 + B12)$
  - Clay/Alteration Overburden Index: $\text{OCI} = B11 / B12$
  - Ferrous Iron Composite Ratio: $\text{FICR} = B12 / B8$
- **Physical Dual-Regime Gating (Gate 2):** Under dense tropical canopy ($\text{NDVI} \ge 0.2$), all SWIR and alteration values ($\text{WCDI}, \text{OCI}, \text{FICR}, \text{Albedo}$) are strictly set to `NaN` (no raw optical canopy reflections leak into training tabular models).
- **Interim Artifact:** `data/kalimantan/interim/s2_dry_season_composite_20m.tif`.
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `spec_ndvi.tif`, `spec_ndre.tif`, `spec_albedo_gated.tif`, `spec_wcdi_gated.tif`, `spec_oci_gated.tif`, `spec_ficr_gated.tif`.

### Workstream 3: Thermal Infrared (Landsat 8/9 TIRS & ASTER TIR)
- **Sensors:** USGS Landsat 8/9 TIRS (Band 10, Surface Kinetic Temperature ST) and Terra ASTER TIR (Bands 10–14).
- **Physics Indices:**
  - Kinetic Temperature Anomaly: $\Delta T = T_{\text{surface}} - T_{\text{mean\_diurnal}}$ (isolating geothermal, coal self-heating, and rock heat capacitance anomalies).
  - Ninomiya Silica Index: $B13 / B11$ (differentiating quartz-rich silicates and hydrothermal silica caps from weathered regolith).
- **Interim Artifacts:** `data/kalimantan/interim/thermal_anomaly_st_30m.tif`, `data/kalimantan/interim/aster_silica_index_90m.tif`.
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `thermal_anomaly_st.tif`, `aster_silica_index.tif`.

### Workstream 4: Digital Elevation & Morphometrics (Copernicus DEM GLO-30)
- **Sensor:** Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30m European Space Agency global elevation).
- **Morphometric Derivatives:**
  - Elevation ($z$), Slope ($\beta = \arctan \sqrt{p^2 + q^2}$), Aspect ($\alpha = \arctan2(-q, p)$).
  - Evans (1980) Plan Curvature ($k_p = -2(D H^2 + E G^2 - F G H) / (G^2 + H^2 + \epsilon)$).
  - Multi-Scale Topographic Position Index: Local TPI ($R=500\text{ m}$) and Regional TPI ($R=2,000\text{ m}$).
  - Synclinal Valley Keel Index: $\text{TPI}_{\text{syncline}} = \max(0, -\text{TPI}_{\text{regional}}) \times (1 - \tanh(\beta / 10^\circ))$ (targeting coal basin accumulation troughs).
  - Riley (1999) Terrain Ruggedness Index (TRI).
  - Distance to Major Topographic Lineaments ($\text{km}$).
  - Distance to Coastal Continental Shelf Edge ($\text{km}$).
  - Regolith Cover Thickness Estimate ($m$) & Outcrop Exposure Index ($[0, 1]$).
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `dem_elevation.tif`, `dem_slope.tif`, `dem_aspect.tif`, `dem_plan_curvature.tif`, `dem_tpi_local.tif`, `dem_tpi_regional.tif`, `dem_tpi_syncline_keel.tif`, `terrain_ruggedness_tri.tif`, `dist_topo_lineaments_km.tif`, `coastal_shelf_dist_km.tif`, `regolith_cover_m.tif`, `outcrop_exposure_index.tif`.

### Workstream 5: Bedrock Geology, Faults & Sediment Thickness
- **Data Ingestion:**
  - Official ESDM Coal-Bearing Formations (1:250,000 scale) retrieved directly from the live ESDM REST MapServer (`Warukin`, `Tanjung`, `Balikpapan`, `Pamaluan`, `Dahor` formations; 170 polygons).
  - GEM Global Active Faults database clipped to the Kalimantan tectonic domain (Meratus suture, Adang-Paternoster fault, Lupar fault, Mangkalihat fault).
  - NOAA GlobSed sediment thickness model.
- **Geomechanical Stress Derivatives:**
  - Cauchy 2D Stress Tensor projections under Regional Compression ($\sigma_1 = 125^\circ\text{ SE}$):
    - Dilation Tendency: $T_d = (\sigma_1 - \sigma_n) / (\sigma_1 - \sigma_3) = \sin^2(\theta - \theta_{\sigma1})$.
    - Slip Tendency: $T_s = \tau / \sigma_n = |\sin(2(\theta - \theta_{\sigma1}))| / 2$.
  - Euclidean Distance to Active Faults ($m$) and Structural Fault Density ($\text{km}^{-2}$).
  - Proximity to Coal Formation Boundaries / Contacts ($\text{km}$).
- **Interim Artifacts:** `data/kalimantan/interim/esdm_geology_250k.gpkg`, `data/kalimantan/interim/kalimantan_faults_gem.geojson`, `data/kalimantan/interim/sediment_thickness_globsed.tif`.
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `dist_active_faults_m.tif`, `active_fault_density.tif`, `dilation_tendency_Td.tif`, `slip_tendency_Ts.tif`, `sediment_thickness_globsed.tif`, `geology_coal_contact_dist_km.tif`.

### Workstream 6: Potential Fields Geophysics
- **Satellite Gravimetry:** Scripps UCSD Sandwell & Smith v33.1 Free-Air satellite altimetry gravity grid, with Bullard $2.67\text{ g/cm}^3$ Bouguer slab correction.
  - Residual Gravity: $30\text{ km}$ Gaussian low-pass spatial separation ($\Delta g_{\text{res}} = g_{\text{bouguer}} - g_{\text{regional}}$).
  - Parker-Oldenburg 3D Fourier Moho Inversion: Inverting regional gravity for Moho depth ($z_0 = 32\text{ km}$, $\Delta \rho = 0.45\text{ g/cm}^3$, cosine ring taper).
  - Moho Gradient Step: Structural crustal thickness step index ($\text{mGal/km}$).
- **Satellite Magnetics:** NOAA EMAG2v3 Total Magnetic Intensity (TMI).
  - MacLeod et al. (1993) Low-Latitude Pseudo-Inclination Equatorial Reduction-To-Pole (RTP) ($|I| < 20^\circ$ stabilization, preserving true north magnetic dipole orientation without equatorial division-by-zero ringing).
  - 3D Total Analytic Signal: $|\text{AS}| = \sqrt{(\partial B/\partial x)^2 + (\partial B/\partial y)^2 + (\partial B/\partial z)^2}$.
- **Interim Artifacts:** Ingested Sandwell & Smith v33.1 and EMAG2v3 GeoTIFFs.
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `bouguer_gravity_mgal.tif`, `gravity_residual_30km_mgal.tif`, `crustal_thickness_km.tif`, `moho_gravity_step_mgal_km.tif`, `emag2_rtp_magnetics.tif`, `emag2_analytic_signal.tif`.

### Workstream 7: Ground-Truth Deposits & Selection Bias Correction
- **Ground-Truth Target Sets:**
  - ESDM Coal Catalog (850 sites) & ESDM Iron Catalog (79 sites) from the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources of Indonesia.
  - Borneo $K=4$ Metallic Reference Catalog (Porphyry Cu, Skarn Fe-Cu, Epithermal Au, Laterite Ni).
- **Centroid Purge (Gate 3):** Quarantined the USGS MRDS island centroid artifact at `(-1.9982° S, 113.9995° E)` (radius $0.08^\circ \approx 9\text{ km}$) preventing fake central Kalimantan coordinate dumps from poisoning training labels.
- **Observation / Logistics Bias Covariates:**
  - OSM Logistics & Navigable River Networks: Distance to major rivers (Mahakam, Barito, Kapuas, Kahayan, Kayan) and roads ($\text{km}$).
  - Global Forest Watch (GFW) Annual Forest Canopy Loss & Disturbance Rate ($[0, 1]$).
- **Harmonized 250m Outputs:** `dist_transport_km.tif`, `forest_disturbance_rate.tif`, `label_coal.tif`, `label_k0_porphyry_cu.tif`, `label_k1_skarn_fe_cu.tif`, `label_k2_epithermal_au.tif`, `label_k3_laterite_fe_ni.tif`, `alteration_benchmark.tif`, `explored_footprint.tif`.
