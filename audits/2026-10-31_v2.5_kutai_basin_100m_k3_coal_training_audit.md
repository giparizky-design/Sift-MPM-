## 1. Executive Summary & Geological Context

This audit evaluates the complete pipeline execution, multi-round incremental training, physical feature fidelity, and target extraction for the entire onshore **Kutai Basin** at $100\text{ m}$ Ultra-HD spatial resolution.

The Kutai Basin represents Indonesia's premier Tertiary sedimentary basin, characterized by thick Oligocene to Miocene deltaic, paralic, and syn-rift sequences with intensive anticlinal fold-thrust trends (Samarinda Anticlinorium). Prior mineral prospectivity scans either evaluated magmatic-hydrothermal metallic systems ($K=4$) or covered coal only at regional $250\text{ m}$ and $500\text{ m}$ decimation. This run establishes the first wall-to-wall $100\text{ m}$ single-domain $K=3$ coal prospectivity baseline across the entire basin extent $[115.0^\circ\text{E}, -2.0^\circ\text{S}]$ to $[118.0^\circ\text{E}, 1.4^\circ\text{N}]$.
