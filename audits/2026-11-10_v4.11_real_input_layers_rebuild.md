# v4.11 — Real Input Layers Rebuild (no fabricated data)

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Scope:** Every input layer of the Kalimantan island cube and the East Kalimantan (EK) cube.
**Trigger:** The owner asked for real Sentinel-2, thermal, EMAG2 magnetics and ALOS PALSAR inputs, cohesive across datasets, with no synthesized data.

## 2. What replaced them

New, non-destructive cubes built by `scripts/fetch_real_layers.py` and `scripts/build_real_feature_cubes.py`:

* `data/kalimantan_real/processed`: the island master grid. EPSG:4326, 0.0025° (~250 m), 3600×4400, **8,586,975 land cells, 78 layers**.
* `data/east_kalimantan_real/processed`: an exact window of the master (rows 520–2920, cols 2400–4400), **2,903,598 land cells**. All 78 EK rasters equal the island rasters cell for cell in the overlap. EK and island now share one recipe, which closes the v4.6 fault/stress mismatch.

Each directory has a `layer_provenance.json` that records every layer as `measured` (a resampled real product, with source URL, scene/item IDs, dates and native resolution) or `derived` (a documented formula whose inputs must already be registered). The build refuses to write a layer without a record. Missing data are NaN.

## 3. Defects found in the first real build and fixed before release

Value-range checks on the first build (`build.log`) found:

1. **S2 ratio blow-ups.** Composite reflectance near zero or negative (deep water, shadow, residue from the −1000 DN offset) gave NDVI from −5.4e5 to 64 and negative albedo. Fix: `sentinel2.MIN_REFLECTANCE = 0.005`; darker bands are NaN (≤0.12% of land cells per band). NDVI is now within [−0.91, 0.97].
2. **Landsat cloud residue.** Cloud tops passed QA_PIXEL: LST reached −20 °C and the anomaly −52 K. Fix: `landsat_thermal.MIN_SEA_LEVEL_LST_C = 18`; cells colder than that after lapse correction are NaN (0.07% of land; the 0.1st percentile was 20 °C). The anomaly is now −16 to +39 K; the hot tail (fires, mine heat) is kept.
3. **`volcanic_arc_halo` was identically 0.** The nearest Holocene volcano is ≥245 km from every land cell, while the halo σ is 35 km. The layer is removed; `dist_volcanic_arc_km` stays.
4. **`emag2_error_nt` held negative fill codes.** Now NaN (quality layer, not a feature).

The rebuild with `--reuse-potential-fields` (which re-registers the unchanged gravity/magnetics/sediment/bedrock rasters from the previous manifest) took 15.5 min. Regression tests: `test_s2_indices_mask_dark_reflectance`, `test_landsat_cold_cloud_residue_masked`.

## 4. Verification

* `assert_feature_provenance` passes on both cubes: all 50 feature columns are registered (33 measured sources, 64 derived records).
* `verify_no_raw_pixel_leakage` passes. Raw `s2_b*` bands are registered as provenance inputs only and are not written to the parquet.
* EK window equals the island cube for all 78 rasters (`np.array_equal`, NaN-aware).
* An AST test confirms no layer builder (14 modules) calls `np.random` / `default_rng`. The legacy fabricating functions in `harvest_kalimantan_multimodal.py` now raise.
* `tests/unit/test_real_sources.py`: 24 passed. Full suite after the first part of this change: 283 passed.
* The training orchestrator (`run_seven_pass_training.py`) and `run_deposit_cv.py` now refuse a dataset without full provenance unless `--allow-unverified-layers` / `MPM_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_LAYERS=1` is set. **The old `data/kalimantan/processed` and `data/east_kalimantan/processed` therefore fail the gate**; they are untouched on disk.

## 5. Caveats (real-data limits, not fabrication)

* **EMAG2v3 is patchy** over Borneo: 36.5% of land (EK 28.5%). The FFT-derived RTP/AS/tilt use a zero-filled field inside the transform and are masked back to the data footprint, so they ring near data edges. EMAG2 is a ~3.7 km regional field and cannot resolve district-scale intrusions.
* **The canopy gate makes optical SWIR and ASTER sparse** (EK: 6–7% S2, 3% ASTER), and those cells are mostly clearings, mines and roads. They are partly an **exploration/disturbance footprint proxy** and can leak "where people already dig". Check feature importance before trusting them.
* ASTER TIR comes from 2000–2006 (SWIR detectors failed in 2008), so archive gaps leave about 67% of land with no scene.
* OSM completeness in interior Borneo is uneven; the transport distances measure distance to *mapped* features.
* `slip_tendency_Ts` / `dilation_tendency_Td` use an assumed regional stress tensor (σ1 az 20°), not a measured one.
* GeoRIMA rock nodes carry exploration-sampling bias. Lithology distances are capped at 15 km, and `dist_pluton_carbonate_contact_km` is 15 km almost everywhere.
* SoilGrids depth to bedrock is a machine-learning product trained on boreholes and soil profiles (a modelled observation), with 15.7% NaN island-wide.
* `outcrop_exposure_index` is a DEM terrain index, not mapped outcrop (named as such in provenance).
* `scripts/run_viewport_7pass.py` still creates a placeholder output polygon; out of scope here and not an input layer.
