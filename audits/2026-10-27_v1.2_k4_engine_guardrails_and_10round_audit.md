## 1. Ground-Truth Guardrails & Physical Reconciliations Audited

The system design enforces six core physical and empirical guardrails across all 7 passes:

| # | Guardrail / Trap | Physical & Dataset Reality | Engineering Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Canopy & Sensor Leakage** | Equatorial rainforest blocks optical/SWIR; raw pixel bands (`b11`, `b13`, `aster_b`, `s2_b`) cause spurious artifacts. | Strictly enforced potential-field petrophysical gates (`mag_analytic_signal_texture * geophys_contrast_4km_mag` for $k=0,1,2$; `plateau_low_slope_gate * bouguer_15km_high` for $k=3$). [`verify_no_raw_pixel_leakage`](file:///Users/gip/Geospatials/src/models/pu_pipeline.py#L78-L133) guards against sensor leakage. |
| **2** | **Zero Barren Drillholes ($RN = \emptyset$)** | Kalimantan has zero confirmed negative drillholes (`label 0 = 0`; `label 1 = 3,163`; `label -1 = 416,385`). | Uses binary $\{1, -1\}$ SAR-nnPU framing with non-negative risk clamping and `max_delta_step = 1.0` in tree leaves to restore overfitted unlabeled margins. |
| **3** | **Placers vs Primary Ore** | 35% of Borneo gold records in colonial records are river gravel placers (`"Emas Alluvial"`). Centroid defaults corrupt coordinates. | Hard-excludes `"Emas Alluvial"` and `is_centroid_artifact == True` from positive sets ($P_k$). Retains alluvial footprints inside pooled human exploration propensity ($\mathcal{E}_{\text{pooled}}$). |
| **4** | **Independent Deposit Mechanics** | `XGBClassifier` rejects 2D sample weights, and joint trees induce negative feature transfer between porphyry and laterite. | 4 Independent XGBoost Heads with dedicated 1D sample weights $\mathbf{w}_k$, objective $P\text{--}A$ evidential feature weighting, and telescoping out-of-fold probability stacking into Head 0 (`Porphyry_Cu_Au`). |
| **5** | **Propensity Polarity** | Dividing by $\hat{e}_{\text{logistics}}$ in Pass 4 after weighting by $1/\hat{e}_{\text{logistics}}$ in Pass 3 double-penalizes explored ground. | Asymmetric sample weighting: positive deposits receive $1/\hat{e}_{\text{logistics}}$, unlabeled ground receives $M \times (0.25 + 0.75 \hat{e}_{\text{logistics}})$. Pass 4 scales strictly by held-out scalar prior $\hat{c}_k$ and OUTMOGC boundary gates $\Gamma_{\text{closed}}^{(k)}(\mathbf{s})$. |
| **6** | **Bouguer Reduction & Moho Inversion** | Satellite gravity is Free-Air ($\Delta g_{\text{FA}}$); synthetic DEM Moho depth is unphysical. | Bouguer slab reduction ($\Delta g_B = \Delta g_{\text{FA}} - 0.1119 \, h_{\text{DEM}}$) and 30 km low-pass gravity Moho inversion: $\text{clip}(32.0 - \Delta g_{B, 30\text{km}} / 16.76, 18.0, 45.0)\ \text{km}$. |
