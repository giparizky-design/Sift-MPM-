> 1. **Every audit** conducted on the codebase, data layers, physics engine, or training runs MUST be added to [`audits/`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/audits/) with semantic version numbering and ISO date (`YYYY-MM-DD_vX.Y_<slug>.md`) and registered in the table below.
