## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

This audit documents the successful execution of an end-to-end continuous incremental training and camp-scale discovery pipeline on East Kalimantan. Starting from model checkpoint **`EXP_EK_250M_SANGATTA_CORRIDOR_V1`**, the system:
1. Executed **10 consecutive training rounds** at $250\text{ m}$ resolution exclusively on the $K=4$ metallic domain (Porphyry Cu-Au, Skarn Fe-Cu, Epithermal Au-Ag, Laterite Fe-Ni), completely bypassing coal to ensure pure metallic domain specialization.
2. Filtered and polygonized the resulting regional prospectivity surface, identifying **5 geographically distinct discovery hotspots** separated by $\ge 25\text{ km}$.
3. Synthesized **$50\text{ m}$ Ultra-HD feature cubes** ($400 \times 400$ cells, 21 leak-free geomechanical, geophysical, and morphometric channels) across each of the 5 hotspots.
4. Warm-started directly from the 10-round Stage-1 model weights to execute **3 focused incremental training rounds** on the $50\text{ m}$ hotspot domain, accumulating booster trees to 650 cumulative trees and adapting the DAE-U-Net neural spatial context to camp-scale structural geology.
5. Re-compiled and validated the interactive Control Center UI bundle with updated weights, conformal bounds, and drill-scale target site cards.

## 4. Stage 3 & 4: Focused $50\text{ m}$ High-Resolution 3-Round Incremental Training

### 4.1 $50\text{ m}$ Feature Cube Synthesis
Each hotspot was synthesized at $50.0\text{ m}$ spatial resolution ($400 \times 400$ cells, $20\text{ km} \times 20\text{ km}$ window) with 21 physical layers:
- **Tectonics & Faults:** Distance to active faults (`dist_active_faults_m`), active fault density (`active_fault_density`), distance to lineaments (`dist_topo_lineaments_km`).
- **Geomechanics:** Slip tendency ($T_s$), Dilation tendency ($T_d$).
- **Geophysics:** Satellite Bouguer gravity anomaly (`bouguer_gravity_mgal`), Moho gravity gradient step (`moho_gravity_step_mgal_km`), crustal thickness (`crustal_thickness_km`).
- **Geomorphology:** SRTM GLO-30 DEM elevation, slope, aspect, local TPI, regional TPI, plan curvature, terrain ruggedness (TRI).
- **Regolith & Cover:** Outcrop exposure index, inferred regolith depth ($2\text{ m}$–$95\text{ m}$).
