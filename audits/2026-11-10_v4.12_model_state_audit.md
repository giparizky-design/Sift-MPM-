# v4.12: State of the Model (East Kalimantan K=4 metals, K=3 coal, DAE U-Net)

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Timestamp:** 2026-11-10T13:35:00+08:00 · **Type:** Training / evaluation audit. No model, data or pipeline code was changed.

**Scope:**
- The deployed East Kalimantan run in `outputs/seven_pass_east_kalimantan/` (trained 2026-11-09 20:16, v4.8 defaults, U-Net off).
- The old cube it was trained on (`data/east_kalimantan/processed`) against the v4.11 real cube (`data/east_kalimantan_real/processed`).
- The status of the v4.9, v4.10 and v4.11 findings in the current code.
- New deposit-level runs: 21 runs under [`outputs/deposit_cv/v412/`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/outputs/deposit_cv/v412/) (scripts `run_v412.sh`, `run_v412_b.sh`, `run_v412_seeds.sh`).

All AUCs below are **median-cell** success-rate AUCs (v4.10 finding 1) at a 15 km buffer, 5 folds, on held-out deposits. Unit tests: 285 passed.

## Summary

1. **Nothing has been retrained since v4.8.** The deployed models were trained on the old cube, which now fails the provenance gate. All v4.10 findings are still open.
2. **The deployed metals model is worse than the one that was evaluated.** It includes six SAR channels; with them it scores 0.844 against 0.870 without (mean of 4 seeds; worse in every seed).
3. **The real cube neither helps nor hurts metals on the same 14 features** (0.861 vs 0.870). A retrain with the default feature selection on the real cube would use 44 features and score 0.813.
4. **A logistic regression matches XGBoost on the real cube** (0.867 vs 0.858). The limit is the 33 deposits, not model capacity.
5. **Coal on the real cube cannot be evaluated as it stands.** The coal labels are generated from feature layers, almost none of them coincide between the two cubes, and one real-cube feature reproduces the label rule (AUC 0.965 is circular).
6. **The U-Net is out of the deployed model but on by default** for the next retrain, in both the script and the dashboard.
7. **Site-card numbers are not what their names say.** Every target comes from a fallback path; "lower bound" equals the map value, and for coal the "upper bound" is below the point estimate on 65% of cells.

### Findings

**2.1 The deployed metals model includes SAR channels that cost AUC (High).**
- v4.10 finding 3 noted the deployed 20-feature model had never been scored. It is now scored: −0.026 on "any", the same sign in all four seeds. Porphyry loses most (−0.059).
- Seed 42 paired: −0.029 [−0.058, +0.000], 21 of 33 deposits worse.
- The loss repeats on the real cube (14 → 14 + SAR: 0.858 → 0.808).
- The archived PR-AUC and v4.8 numbers describe the 14-feature model, not this one.

**2.2 A default retrain on the real cube would lose about 0.05 (High).**
- `select_feature_cols` takes every non-excluded column: 44 after the metals regional drop.
- Removing the 11 surface layers does not recover it, so the v4.11 "exploration-footprint proxy" concern is not the main cause for metals. The added regional and lithology layers are.
- The real cube also carries regional fields not on the v4.8 drop list (`gravity_residual_30km_mgal`, `gravity_residual_basin_mgal`, `sediment_thickness_m`, EMAG2). They were not tested one by one.

**2.3 A linear model matches the ensemble (Medium).**
- Real cube, 14 features: logistic regression 0.867, XGBoost 0.858. Old cube: 0.811 vs 0.857.
- With 33 deposits, extra capacity (more features, deeper models, the U-Net) is not what limits the score.

**2.4 Epithermal is weak everywhere (High, open from v4.10 finding 2).**
- 0.62–0.73 in all 16 runs; no held-out epithermal deposit lands in the top 5% of area by median cell in most runs.
- Yet 10 of the 30 deployed metals targets are labelled epithermal.

**2.5 Seed variation is small relative to the effects above.** SD of "any" over 4 seeds is 0.004–0.011. Per-head SDs reach 0.03–0.10 (epithermal on 44 features), so per-head differences below about 0.05 are not resolved.

## 3. Coal

**3.1 The coal rank labels are generated, not observed (High).**
- `build_k3_coal_rank_targets` steps 1–3.5 km from each concession point along the SAR bedding strike and labels cells that (a) lie in the same mapped formation polygon and (b) have intact canopy (NDVI ≥ 0.25, HH ≤ −2 dB, no mine, low forest loss).
- The three rank heads are trained and scored on these cells. The observed coal data (ESDM points, as 1 km disks in `label_coal`) are only used to exclude negatives.
- Earlier audits describe the rule (v3.1) and its negatives problem (v4.8) but none states that the coal AUC measures agreement with this rule.

**3.2 The labels do not survive a rebuild (High).**

| Head | Old cube cells | Real cube cells | In both |
|---|---|---|---|
| High CV | 621 | 192 | 10 |
| Medium CV | 514 | 104 | 0 |
| Low CV | 1,096 | 365 | 15 |

- Label clusters fall from 361 to 176.
- v4.11 §2 says labels are unchanged. That holds for the K=4 metals labels and the `label_coal` disks (33,301 of 33,902 shared), not for the rank heads.
- The deployed coal model was trained on the old set.

**3.3 On the real cube, one feature reproduces the label rule (High).**

| Run (real cube, gated, 176 clusters) | Any AUC |
|---|---|
| Like-for-like 20 features | 0.691 |
| Same, logistic regression | 0.754 |
| Default selection (50 features) | **0.965** |
| Default minus surface layers | 0.964 |

- `dist_sedimentary_polygon_km` is 0 for 100% of positives and for 9.5% of background cells. Alone it gives a cell-level AUC of 0.952.
- It is the distance to the same ESDM polygons that the label rule requires positives to be inside. The 0.965 is the rule read back.
- Only 39.7% of the observed coal disks lie inside those polygons. A model that learns "inside the polygon" would score well here and miss most known coal.
- `dist_marine_deltaic_contact_km` (cell AUC 0.90) comes from the same polygons.

**3.4 The deployed coal feature list is equivalent to the evaluated one (resolved).**
- Old cube, deployed 27-feature list: 0.774, against 0.770 for the 21-feature C4 run. Paired +0.004 [−0.007, +0.015].
- Per head: low CV −0.018 [−0.032, −0.004]; the others are within noise.

**3.5 The coal baseline on comparable features fell from 0.770 to 0.691 on the real cube**, and logistic regression beats XGBoost there (0.754). Because the label sets differ (3.2), this is not a like-for-like regression and should not be read as one.

## 4. DAE U-Net

- **Not in the deployed model.** Weight 0, no checkpoints in the run, and both `dae_reconstruction_error_*.tif` rasters are entirely NaN.
- **v4.9 numbers recomputed from `per_deposit.csv`; they match.** U-Net alone 0.702 (metals) and 0.626 (coal) against 0.857 and 0.770. At 15 epochs: 0.575 and 0.569. The only harmless blend has weight ≈ 0.
- **Still on by default (Medium, new).** `disable_unet=False` in `run_seven_pass_training.py`; the dashboard checkbox `chk-train-unet` is ticked. The command in AGENTS.md §5 (`--region EAST_KALIMANTAN --reset`) re-enables it with the PR-AUC-proportional blend that v4.9 showed loses AUC, and fallback weights 0.30 / 0.25.
- **Code defects confirmed in `src/models/unet_model.py`:** decimation hard-codes `native_res_m=50`; all ASPP dilations round to 1 on this grid; the fourth dilation is computed and never used. AGENTS.md §4.7 is inert here.
- **Not tested:** the U-Net on the real cube, or with SAR channels.

## 5. Outputs: maps, targets and site cards

**5.1 Every deployed target comes from the fallback path (High).**
- The lower-bound raster is near zero everywhere (metals max 0.043; 0.002% of cells above 0).
- The primary rule (`L ≥ threshold`, threshold clipped to ≥ 0.35) therefore finds nothing. The fallback thresholds the calibrated map at its 96th percentile and passes the calibrated map in as the "lower bound".
- Result: on all 60 cards `lower_bound_L` equals `calibrated_deposit_prob`. The printed "90% CI" has a median width of 0.007 (metals) and 0.012 (coal).

**5.2 Coal upper bound below the point estimate (High).** On 65% of coal map cells `upper_bound_U` < the calibrated value. Cards show it directly (for example rank 21: L 0.856, U 0.777).

**5.3 `calibrated_deposit_prob` is not a probability (High, open from v4.10 finding 4).** ĉ is still on the 0.10 floor for all metal heads. Metals cards show 0.46–0.74 where the raw score is 0.05–0.17.

**5.4 Target geometry (Medium).**
- Metals target 1 is a single 673 km² polygon (77% of all metals target area), classed brownfield.
- 13 of 30 coal targets are one grid cell (0.077 km²). The 30 coal targets total 7.3 km².

**5.5 Ranking uses unvalidated terms (Medium).** The metals `rank_score` is 0.40 × probability + 0.25 × dilation tendency + 0.20 × alteration − 0.15 × cover depth. Alteration is absent in this run, cover depth is the DEM heuristic, and dilation tendency uses an assumed stress tensor. `dilation_tendency_Td` is also the top feature by gain in the porphyry and skarn heads (36–38%), so it is counted twice.

**5.6 Greenfield targets sit on the propensity floor.** 10 of 15 greenfield metals targets have e(x) = 0.050–0.051, the clamp value.

## 7. Checked and found sound

- K=4 metal labels are identical between the two cubes (556 / 719 / 615 / 55 cells).
- The real EK cube is on the same grid as the old one, so paired comparison by deposit is valid (32–33 of 33 match).
- The provenance gate works: the old cube is refused without the override; the real cube passes.
- Unit tests: 285 passed in 372 s.
- The harness reproduces: `unet/xgb_*` equals M13 exactly (v4.9), and seed-to-seed SD on "any" is ≤ 0.011.

## 8. Limits of this audit

- East Kalimantan only. The island-wide real cube (168 deposits) was not run.
- All metals comparisons use the same 33 deposits that v4.8 was tuned on. Absolute AUCs stay optimistic.
- Median-cell AUC removes the max-cell bias but the harness null is still not fixed in code.
- The individual real-cube layers were not ablated one by one; only groups.
- U-Net checkpoints in the older Kelian, Kutai and tile runs were not examined.

## 9. Recommendations (priority order)

1. **Do not retrain production on the real cube with default feature selection.** Pin an explicit metals feature list (the 14, with `depth_to_bedrock_m`) and make production and the harness read the same list.
2. **Remove the six SAR channels from the metals model** on the next retrain. Keep them for coal only if a valid coal test supports it.
3. **Fix the coal test before any coal retrain.** Score against observed coal (the ESDM points, held out by block), and exclude `dist_sedimentary_polygon_km`, `dist_marine_deltaic_contact_km` and any other layer built from the label rule's inputs.
4. **Make the U-Net off by default** in `run_seven_pass_training.py` and the dashboard; set the fallback weights to 0.
5. **Rename or remove the card fields** `calibrated_deposit_prob`, `conformal_90_ci` and `conformal_90pct_target_set`; present area percentiles instead. Fix the coal upper bound.
6. **Cap target polygon size and drop single-cell targets.**
7. **Ablate the new real-cube layers one group at a time** (magnetics, residual gravity, lithology distances) before admitting any of them.
8. **Then** the open v4.10 items: harness null, epithermal, training on all deposits, a lockbox on island-wide deposits outside EK.
