**Date:** 2026-11-09 · **Timestamp:** 2026-11-09T22:30:00+08:00 · **Type:** Training / evaluation audit (review only, no code changed)
**Numbering:** v4.9 is left for the deposit-level U-Net test that was running at the time (`outputs/deposit_cv/v49/`).

**Scope:**

- The deposit-level harness ([`src/evaluation/deposit_cv.py`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/src/evaluation/deposit_cv.py), [`scripts/run_deposit_cv.py`](file:///Volumes/Seagate%202/Geospatials/scripts/run_deposit_cv.py)).

## Summary
- **The deposit-level harness is sound in design.** Every preprocessing step is refit per fold. Folds are grouped by block, held-out deposits are buffered, and the runs include baselines and paired tests. It is the right acceptance test.
- **Six problems change how the current results should be read:**
  1. The success-rate metric's random baseline is not 0.5.
  2. The epithermal head got worse under v4.8.
  3. The deployed metals model is not the configuration that was evaluated.
  4. Production withholds a fold of deposits for a calibration step that ends up on its clamp floor.
  5. The "conformal" outputs carry no coverage guarantee.
  6. The +0.05 v4.8 gain was selected on its own test folds.

## Findings

### 1. The success-rate AUC null is about 0.65 for metals, not 0.5 (High)
**Cause.**
- A held-out deposit is scored by the **maximum** over its cells. A metals cluster has a median of 56 cells, because of the 1 km disk rasterisation.
- That maximum is compared with **single** background cells (`deposit_area_fractions`).
- So a random location already scores above most of the background.
- `summarize_success_rate` still tests against Uniform(0, 1) area fractions.

**Measured on the production maps.** Random disks were dropped on each map and scored the same way.

| Map | Disk size (cells) | Null AUC | Random disks landing in the "top 5%" |
|---|---|---|---|
| metals | 57 (metal cluster size) | **0.651** | **21.6%** |
| metals | 29 | 0.625 | 17.1% |
| metals | 9 | 0.563 | 11.0% |
| coal | 9 (coal clusters have a median of 6 cells) | 0.570 | 8.7% |

**Effect.**
- The reported p-values against a random map are too small.
- The capture lifts are overstated about 4× for metals. For example, "82% of held-out deposits at 5% of area" should be read against about 22%, not 5%.
- Comparisons between runs are less affected, because all runs share the bias. Rougher maps inflate more, though, so a change that makes the map rougher can look better than it is.

**Median-cell AUC.** The median-cell variant, already written to `per_deposit.csv`, is free of this bias.

| Run | Variant | Max-cell AUC | Median-cell AUC |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0b baseline | raw | 0.866 | 0.810 |
| M0b baseline | calibrated | 0.873 | 0.816 |
| M13 adopted | raw | 0.927 | **0.857** |
| M13 adopted | calibrated | 0.925 | **0.852** |
| C0 coal baseline | gated | 0.800 | 0.753 |
| C4 coal adopted | gated | 0.814 | **0.770** |

M13 still beats the baseline on median-cell AUC (+0.04 to +0.05), so the v4.8 direction holds. The absolute numbers to quote are about 0.85 for metals and 0.77 for coal, not 0.93 and 0.81.

**Fix (not applied).**
- Score background cells with a maximum filter of the same footprint as the cluster, so like is compared with like.
- Or compute the null by permuting disk placement.
- Report median-cell AUC as the headline.

**Caveat.** The null was measured on the production maps, not on the per-fold maps, and its size depends on how rough a map is.

### 2. Epithermal got worse under v4.8; max-cell scoring hid it (High)

| Epithermal (11 deposits) | Max-cell AUC | Median-cell AUC |
|---|---|---|
| M0b, raw | 0.868 | 0.778 |
| M13, raw | 0.808 | **0.683** (−0.095) |
| M0b, calibrated | 0.806 | 0.681 |
| M13, calibrated | 0.819 | **0.624** (−0.057) |

- The "any metal" gain comes from porphyry and skarn. Epithermal deposits in "any" are still caught by the other heads.
- Production uses the epithermal head on its own: for target classes, for site cards and in 22–35% of the composite map's top cells.
- v4.8 noted that epithermal "does not gain". The median-cell scores show that it in fact lost.
- **Recommendation.** Keep the regional fields for the epithermal head only, or test residual gravity for it (v4.8 recommendation 3). Accept per head, not only on "any".

### 3. The deployed metals model is not the configuration that was evaluated (High)
- **Production.** `k4_ensemble.joblib` has `feature_names_` = 27 columns and uses **20**: the 14 kept by M13 plus the six PALSAR-2 SAR channels (`sar_palsar2_hh/hv/dpr/lineament_density`, `sar_bedding_strike_deg`, `sar_glcm_anisotropy`).
- **v4.8 M13** was evaluated with `features_k4_ensemble.txt`: 21 columns minus 7 = **14**, with no SAR.
- **The running v4.9 U-Net test** uses the same 14.
- **What exists for SAR.** The only deposit-level SAR evidence is from v4.5, under the old v4.7 settings (no gain).
- **Not evaluated.** The 20-feature v4.8 model has never been scored at deposit level.
- **Feature encoding.** `sar_bedding_strike_deg` and `dem_aspect` are circular variables (0° = 360°) used as raw degrees. Encode them as sin/cos, or as axial 2θ for strike.
- **Not checked.** The provenance of the EK SAR channels (real PALSAR-2 or synthetic) was not checked. The v4.4 audit found synthetic SAR elsewhere.

### 4. Production withholds about 22% of training rows for a calibration that sits on its clamp floor (High)
- **Withheld data.** The production K=4 ensemble is fit on `X_all[train_idx]` (L1331–1343): 90,842 of 117,196 rows. One spatial fold, and its deposits, is held back so that ĉ and q̂ can be estimated on it. Coal does the same with `tr_coal_idx`. No final refit on all data was found.
- **ĉ hits the floor.**
  - Metals: ĉ = **0.10, 0.10, 0.10, 0.10** (all on the `min_c=0.10` floor).
  - Coal: ĉ = 0.10, 0.10, 0.123.
  - The ensemble's own internal `c_hat_` disagrees (0.24 / 0.26 / 0.17 / 0.12).
- **What "calibrated" means as a result.**
  - The "calibrated prospectivity" is `min(1, 10 × raw) × gate`: a monotone rescale, not a probability.
  - 0.54% of metal cells sit at exactly 1.0, so the top of the map is a block of ties.
- **The method does not apply as used.** Elkan–Noto needs a classifier trained on labelled vs. unlabeled data at natural prevalence. Here the classifier has propensity and Hájek weights, an 80k background subsample and exclusion halos.
- **Recommendation.**
  - Train production on all deposits.
  - Present the maps as relative scores or area percentiles. The harness already measures that scale.
  - If probabilities are needed, calibrate on deposit-level out-of-fold scores from the harness, not on one pixel fold.

### 5. The conformal outputs carry no coverage guarantee (Medium)
**q̂ is degenerate.**
- Metals q̂_90 = 0.9987 / 0.9265 / **1.005** / 0.9903.
- The set gate `p − 0.5σ ≥ 1 − q̂` therefore has a threshold near 0.
- Share of the map passing the gate (upper bound, σ ignored): porphyry 56%, **epithermal 100%**, skarn 6.5%, laterite 7.4%.

**Why there is no guarantee.**
- **Non-exchangeable calibration points.** The calibration points are pixels from one fold: a few deposits × about 56 strongly correlated cells.
- **Laterite** has no validation positive. Its "calibration" falls back to the map's 95th percentile (L1541–1543).

**The site-card sets come from somewhere else.**
- `conformal_90pct_target_set` on the site cards is not produced by this procedure.
- It is the heads with score ≥ `eff_l_thr`, falling back to the argmax head (`run_seven_pass_training.py` L2264–2268).
- 29 of 30 production cards show a single head.

**Recommendation.** Rename the field (for example `heads_above_threshold`), or compute deposit-level conformal sets from the harness's out-of-fold scores. This falls under the provenance-honesty rule in AGENTS §3.3.

### 6. The v4.8 gain was selected on the test folds and ignores fold-assignment variance (Medium)
- **Selection.** M13 was chosen after 14 metals runs on the same 33 deposits and the same fold assignment (`random_state=42`). v4.8 already notes this.
- **Missing variance.** The bootstrap CI resamples deposits only. It leaves out the variance from fold assignment and from training seeds.
- **Recommendation.**
  - Re-run M0b and M13 with 5–10 fold seeds and report the spread of Δ.
  - Freeze a lockbox for the next decision: island-wide deposits outside EK, after the fault, stress and SAR recipes are unified (v4.6). Touch it once.

### 7. Smaller items
- **Laterite in the composite.** Laterite (one deposit, 55 cells) is still trained and enters the composite and the targets. It drives 0.9% of the top 1% and 3% of the top 5% of the composite metals map. Mask it out of the composite until it has at least 5 deposits, consistent with the `insufficient_data` flag.
- **Stale metrics.** `seven_pass_summary.json` still reports the in-script OOF pixel PR-AUC (metals 0.047, coal 0.026). That loop reuses production-fold masks (v4.5). `blind_holdout.json` (2026-11-08) is a copy of those OOF numbers. Replace both with the harness summary, or label them clearly.
- **Small samples.** The frontier AUC uses n = 14 deposits; porphyry/skarn/epithermal have 10/13/11. Treat per-head numbers as indicative.

## Checked and found sound
- **Harness design:**
  - per-fold refit of propensity, exclusion masks, weights, gates, distance caps and Elkan–Noto;
  - block-grouped folds;
  - a 15 km buffer;
  - logistic-regression and single-feature baselines;
  - paired Wilcoxon and bootstrap comparisons.
- **No raw-band or label-derived features** in the production feature lists. The v2.6 coal leaks are absent.
- **Fixed seeds** (`random_state=42`) in the ensembles and fold assignment.
- **Class imbalance** is handled by PU weighting, Hájek-normalised positives and a background subsample. The trees are regularised: depth 6, `min_child_weight` 10, 5 bags.

## Recommendations (priority order)
1. Fix the harness null (finding 1) and make median-cell AUC the headline metric. Re-baseline the v4.8 tables.
2. Evaluate the deployed 20-feature model at deposit level, then make production and the harness read one feature list (finding 3).
3. Remove the epithermal regression (finding 2) and accept changes per head.
4. Train production on all deposits, and drop or relabel the Elkan–Noto and conformal outputs (findings 4 and 5).
5. Re-run with repeated fold seeds, and set up a lockbox (finding 6).
