## 2. Mathematical Discrepancies Resolution (D1–D10)

### 2.1 D1 & D9: Conformal Quantile Prediction Set Gate Decoupled from Studentized Lower Bound
- **Prior Flaw:** The pipeline previously conflated two distinct conformal metrics: the multi-target prediction set inclusion threshold $\tau_k = 1 - \hat{q}_{1-\alpha, k}$ and the spatial drill target lower confidence bound $L(x) = \hat{P}(x) - t_{n-1, \alpha} \cdot \sigma(x)$. In streaming inference (`scripts/run_seven_pass_training.py`), lower bound arrays were incorrectly thresholded against $\tau_k$, causing empty prediction sets and double-penalizing high-epistemic-uncertainty pixels.
- **Resolution (v4.0):**
  - **Prediction Set Inclusion Gate $\mathcal{C}_{1-\alpha}(s)$:** Evaluated strictly on calibrated point probabilities:
    $$\mathcal{C}_{1-\alpha}(s) = \left\{k \in \{0, \dots, K-1\} : P_k(s) \ge 1 - \hat{q}_{1-\alpha, k}\right\}$$
    Implemented in `src/models/conformal.py::evaluate_conformal_set_gate` and `build_conformal_target_sets`.
  - **Drill Target Lower Bound $L_k(s)$:** Evaluated independently via Student's $t$ lower bound:
    $$L_k(s) = \max\left(0.0, P_k(s) - t_{n-1, \alpha} \cdot \sigma_k(s)\right)$$
    Implemented in `src/models/conformal.py::compute_studentized_conformal_lower_bound`. Target polygonization in Pass 6 operates on $L_k(s) \ge 0.50$, while Pass 7 site cards record the exact candidate set $\mathcal{C}_{0.90}(s)$.

### 2.2 D2 & Bug 5: Prior-Corrected Bayesian Optical WoE Log-Odds Fusion
- **Prior Flaw:** When fusing the base ensemble prospectivity $P_{\text{ens}}(s)$ with tropical canopy optical SWIR Weight-of-Evidence ($W^+_{\text{swir}}$), the pipeline directly added $W^+_{\text{swir}}$ to $\text{logit}(P_{\text{ens}})$. Because $P_{\text{ens}}$ already incorporates regional background odds $\text{logit}(\pi_k)$, adding $W^+_{\text{swir}} = \ln(P(SWIR | D) / P(SWIR | \bar{D}))$ double-counted the discovery prior, causing runaway log-odds explosion.
- **Resolution (v4.0):** Subtracted the prior log-odds $\text{logit}(\pi_k)$ in evidential log-odds combination:
  $$\text{logit}(P_{\text{fused}}) = \text{logit}(P_{\text{ens}}) + W^+_{\text{swir}} - \text{logit}(\pi_k)$$
  Implemented in `src/models/fusion.py::precision_weighted_log_odds_fusion`. When $W^+_{\text{swir}} = \text{logit}(\pi_k)$, the fusion simplifies to identity: $P_{\text{fused}} = P_{\text{ens}}$.

### 2.3 D3: Hájek Self-Normalized Asymmetric Positive Sample Weights
- **Prior Flaw:** Horvitz-Thompson positive weights $w_i = 1 / e(x_i)$ frequently experienced severe numerical instability when exploration accessibility $e(x_i) \to 0$ in remote rainforest terrain, causing a single inaccessible mine to dominate the gradient.
- **Resolution (v4.0):** Implemented Hájek self-normalized positive weights:
  $$w_i = \frac{1 / e(x_i)}{\sum_{j \in P_k} (1 / e(x_j))}, \quad \forall i \in P_k$$
  Implemented in `src/models/metals.py::compute_hajek_asymmetric_head_weights`. Guarantees $\sum_{i \in P_k} w_i = 1.0$ across all positive samples for head $k$.

### 2.4 D4: Neutral Unlabeled Weights ($w_u = 1.0$) & DAE-UNet Prior Bias Initialization
- **Prior Flaw:** In unlabeled space, setting $w_u = 1 / e(x_u)$ rewarded exploration avoidance in inaccessible areas as "confirmatory negatives," penalizing remote undiscovered deposits.
- **Resolution (v4.0):**
  - Unlabeled weights are strictly set to neutral unit weight: $w_u = 1.0, \forall u \in U$.
  - Classification head biases in `DistrictDAEUNet` are initialized to empirical prior log-odds:
    $$\beta_{0, k} = \text{logit}(\pi_k) = \ln\left(\frac{\pi_k}{1 - \pi_k}\right)$$
    Implemented in `src/models/unet_model.py::DistrictDAEUNet.__init__`. Prevents early training explosion toward 0.50.

### 2.5 D5 & Bug 4: Continuous MacroBasinSequenceGate + Conditional Rank Softmax
- **Prior Flaw:** Single-domain coal prospectivity previously seeded artificial round-robin rank labels across concessions and used discrete step gates with dead feature lookups.
- **Resolution (v4.0):**
  - Continuous basin subsidence gating via `MacroBasinSequenceGate` in `src/models/coal.py`.
  - Hierarchical coal presence $P(\text{Coal})$ is modeled first; conditional coal rank probabilities $P(\text{Rank}_j | \text{Coal})$ are computed via softmax:
    $$P(\text{Rank}_j \cap \text{Coal}) = P(\text{Coal}) \cdot \frac{\exp(z_j)}{\sum_{m=0}^{K-1} \exp(z_m)}$$
    Guarantees the law of total probability: $\sum_{j=0}^{K-1} P(\text{Rank}_j \cap \text{Coal}) \le P(\text{Coal}) \le 1.0$.

### 2.6 D6 & Bug 10: Dimensionally Consistent Physical Scaling in Meters (`px_size_m`)
- **Prior Flaw:** Convolutional receptive fields, morphological closing filters, and buffer distances were hardcoded in pixel counts (e.g. `closing_size = 5`), causing physical filter dimensions to shift by $5\times$ when moving between 250 m, 100 m, and 50 m grids.
- **Resolution (v4.0):** All spatial dimensions are parameterized in physical meters:
  - ASPP bottleneck dilations in `DistrictDAEUNet`:
    $$\text{dilation}_m = \max\left(1, \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{radius}_m}{16.0 \cdot \text{px\_size\_m}}\right)\right), \quad \text{radii} \in [500, 1500, 3000, 6000]\text{ m}$$
  - Morphological closing kernel radius: $R_{\text{px}} = \max(1, \text{round}(R_m / \text{px\_size\_m}))$.
  - Minimum target area: $A_{\text{px}} = \text{round}((A_{\text{ha}} \cdot 10{,}000) / \text{px\_size\_m}^2)$.

### 2.7 D7: Porphyry Cu Concentric Alteration Zonation Features
- **Prior Flaw:** Porphyry copper prospectivity treated hydrothermal alteration indicators as independent isotropic predictors without enforcing Lowell-Guilbert concentric geometry (potassic core $\to$ phyllic halo $\to$ propylitic margin).
- **Resolution (v4.0):** Implemented radial zonation proximity features in `src/models/metals.py::compute_concentric_zonation_features`:
  - `potassic_core_prox`: $1 / (1 + d_{\text{core}} / 500\text{ m})$
  - `phyllic_halo_prox`: Gaussian band at 500–2000 m radius
  - `propylitic_margin_prox`: Sigmoidal outer margin at 2000–5000 m
  - Monotonic directional split constraints enforced in Stage-2 Porphyry meta-stacking head.

### 2.8 D8: Unified 5-Fold Buffered Spatial Cross-Validation Trunk (`src/evaluation/spatial_cv.py`)
- **Prior Flaw:** Cross-validation routines were fragmented across multiple scripts with differing buffer implementations, allowing spatial filter leakage across adjacent grid blocks.
- **Resolution (v4.0):** Unified single trunk in `src/evaluation/spatial_cv.py`:
  - Physical buffer distance: $B_m = \max(\max_k(r^*_k), 16 \cdot \text{px\_size\_m})$.
  - Purges all candidate training samples within $B_m$ of any validation cell:
    $$\text{Train}_{\text{clean}} = \left\{i \in \text{Train}_{\text{cand}} : \min_{j \in \text{Val}} \|x_i - x_j\|_2 \ge B_m\right\}$$
  - Fully compatible with `scikit-learn` via `BufferedSpatialBlockKFold`.

### 2.9 D10: Causal Exploration Propensity Model Restricted to Accessibility Covariates
- **Prior Flaw:** Exploration propensity models previously ingested geological features (such as fault density or lithology), conflating geological prospectivity with human accessibility.
- **Resolution (v4.0):** Propensity models are strictly restricted to `CANONICAL_PROPENSITY_COLS` in `src/schema/canonical.py`:
  - `dist_road_km`, `dist_river_km`, `dist_city_km`, `dist_coast_km`, `slope_deg`, `dem_elevation_m`.
  - Zero geological, geochemical, or geophysical covariates are permitted in propensity fitting.

## 4. Repository Modularization & Backward Compatibility (R3)

To ensure long-term maintainability and modular decoupling, the monolithic `src/models/pu_pipeline.py` (which previously exceeded 2,200 lines) was restructured into focused, single-responsibility modules:

```
src/
├── schema/
│   └── canonical.py          # Centralized feature schemas, deposit types & leakage guard
├── models/
│   ├── calibration.py        # Elkan-Noto calibration, prior estimation & logit scaling
│   ├── coal.py               # MacroBasinSequenceGate & TwoStageK3CoalEnsemble
│   ├── conformal.py          # Conformal prediction set gate & studentized bounds
│   ├── fusion.py             # Bayesian optical WoE log-odds fusion & ensembling
│   ├── metals.py             # TwoStageK4Ensemble, concentric zonation & Hajek weights
│   ├── pu_pipeline.py        # Backward-compatible facade re-exporting all interfaces
│   ├── retrain.py            # Retraining governance & FeedbackLoopError guard
│   ├── training_run_manager.py # Automatic archival & run catalog
│   ├── unet_model.py         # DistrictDAEUNet with ASPP bottleneck & nnPULoss
│   └── xgboost_model.py      # Bagged MPMXGBoost ensemble
└── evaluation/
    ├── metrics.py            # PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, Recall@FPR, NDCG@K
    └── spatial_cv.py         # Unified 5-fold buffered spatial cross-validation trunk
```
