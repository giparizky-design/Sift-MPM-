## Question
The owner wants the K=4 metals and K=3 coal models to find prospective ground away from the labelled deposits, rather than re-finding known ground.

Before this work, 13% of the top 1% of the production metals map lay within 5 km of a known deposit, against 1.3% of the whole map (about 10×). The coal map's top 1% was a cluster about 54 km (median) from any coal label, near row 837, col 644.

## How it was measured
- **Harness.** The v4.5 deposit-level harness, with a **15 km buffer**, 25 km blocks and 5 folds. Production feature lists (21 channels), 100 trees, 5 bags.
- **New metrics:**
  - **Frontier AUC:** success rate over held-out deposits more than 25 km from every training deposit.
  - **Novelty:** the share of the top 1%/5% of background lying within 5/15 km of any known label, against the base rate. It is split into ground near the fold's **training** labels (`xTrain`: re-finding known ground) and near the **held-out** deposits (`xTest`: genuine discovery).
  - **Paired test:** each candidate against the baseline per deposit, with ΔAUC, a bootstrap 95% CI and a Wilcoxon test (`compare_deposit_cv.py`).
- **Acceptance rule.** No significant paired drop in "any" AUC.

## What causes the label-hugging (code findings)
1. **Smooth regional fields act as coordinates for metals.** Bouguer gravity, crustal thickness, the Moho step, plate and arc distances, the arc halo and the coastal-shelf distance vary slowly. Depth-6 trees use them to locate the training deposits. This was the largest effect (below).
2. **Coal negatives were contaminated:**
   - The production subsample keeps all 33,061 `label_coal` 1 km disk cells. These are known coal, but the K3 heads train on strike-line halos, so the disk cells were fed to the heads as **negatives** (`coal.py`, `y_bin = raw_y == 1`).
   - Coal also had no exclusion buffer.
   - Coal multiplied **every** cell by `1/e(x)`, so remote unlabeled cells became negatives with up to about 20× weight.
3. **The metals OUTMOGC gate ran PCA on unscaled features**, so metre-scale columns dominated the envelope.
4. **Production did not use Hájek weights** (`hajek_normalize=False`). Positives got an unnormalised `1/e` (up to 20×), and unlabeled cells got `0.25 + 0.75e`. AGENTS.md §4.6 says this was resolved; it was not true in production until this change.
5. **Rank softmax fed probabilities, not logits.** The coal rank split was near-uniform.
6. **Chunked inference** applied `nan_to_num(0)`, unlike training, and was skipped entirely when the U-Net was off, leaving NaN maps.
7. **Dead rule:** the reliable-negative rule `dist_sed > 15` could never fire after the 15 km cap.
8. **No novelty in targets.** Target ranking had no distance-to-known term, and the top 15 could be all brownfield.

### Explorer claims checked and rejected
- The coal U-Net does **not** weight unlabeled pixels by `1/e`. `nnPU` weights positives only (`unet_model.py` ~L721).
- The porphyry head trains on out-of-fold skarn/epithermal probabilities and predicts with full-fit ones. That is standard stacking, not a leak. Switching it off changed nothing (M4).

- **Capture at 5% of area:** 48% → **82%** of held-out deposits; frontier deposits 21% → **71%**.
- **Per head (calibrated):**
  - porphyry 0.947 → 0.968
  - skarn 0.866 → 0.936
  - epithermal 0.806 → 0.819
  - Raw epithermal drops (0.868 → 0.808, CI includes 0).

**Novelty decomposition, top 1% (raw):**

| Run | Near training labels (5 km) | Near held-out deposits (5 km) | Near held-out deposits (15 km) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0 baseline | 27.9× | 4.9× | 3.6× |
| M13 | 17.9× | 10.6× | 8.7× |

The new model re-finds the ground around its own training deposits much less, and puts about twice as much of its top area around deposits it has never seen.

**Excluded-halo radius does not matter** (M8–M10). Cells within 3.5–6 km of training positives being unlearned is not what drives the hugging.

## Caveats
- **Model selection on the test folds.** M13 was chosen after seeing 14 metals runs on the same 33 deposits and folds, so its +0.05 is optimistic. M7 alone, a single pre-planned change, gives +0.048 [+0.023, +0.073]. The direction is robust, but confirm on independent ground: the island-wide dataset, after the fault, stress and SAR channels are rebuilt with one recipe (see v4.6).
- **Epithermal.** It is the one head that does not gain. Raw epithermal AUC drops when the regional fields go, so they may carry genuine arc-related signal for epithermal.
- **Location surrogates.** The gravity/crust/Moho fields in EK are smooth regional products. Dropping them is the right call for metals here; it is not a statement that crustal architecture is irrelevant. A residual (local minus regional) gravity layer was not tested.
- **Not changed.**
  - The coal map covers 1.72M of 2.92M cells, as before. That looks like the boundary clip and was not investigated.
  - The orchestrator's in-script OOF metrics still reuse production-fold masks (v4.5). Use the harness.
- **Coal depth.** Production coal runs at depth 6, because `--max-depth` is shared between domains. The harness default is 5. The fix was re-checked at depth 6.

## Recommendations
1. Run a deposit-level U-Net test before re-enabling it in production.
2. Rebuild the fault, stress and SAR channels with one recipe, then confirm M13 island-wide with leave-region-out CV.
3. Test residual gravity (25 km high-pass) as a replacement for the dropped regional fields, mainly for epithermal.
4. Add a stratigraphic (formation) input for coal (open since v4.5). Check it for circularity with the same-formation halo rule.
5. Separate `--max-depth` per domain.
