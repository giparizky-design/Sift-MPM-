## Question
Does adding all GeoRIMA metal sites from the whole archipelago, as extra training sites, improve learning?

## Labels
`build_archipelago_k4_targets` applies the production Kalimantan K=4 rules to every island.

| Head | Rules | Points | Positive cells |
|---|---|---|---|
| k0 porphyry | GeoRIMA `COPPER_ORE` / `Tembaga` / `BASE_METALS` / role `porphyry_intrusive`; MRDS Cu | 222 | 184 |
| k1 skarn | GeoRIMA role `carbonate_skarn`; ESDM `Besi Primer` (Kalimantan); GeoRIMA `Besi Primer` elsewhere | 307 | 276 |
| k2 epithermal | GeoRIMA `Emas Primer`; MRDS Au | 398 | 294 |
| k3 laterite | GeoRIMA `NICKEL_COBALT`; ESDM / GeoRIMA `Besi Laterit`; MRDS Ni | 703 | 419 |

- **Hygiene.** Alluvial placers (`Emas Alluvial`) and centroid artifacts are dropped. MRDS rows flagged as centroid or unverified shared coordinates are dropped.
- **Parity with production.** Inside the Borneo bounding box, every production Kalimantan label point is reproduced. The one extra point is a Sulawesi `carbonate_skarn` node at 119.47°E, which production excludes as non-Kalimantan.
- **Points not used.** 17 points fall off the grid or in deep ocean.
- **Regions.** Each cell gets an island group, by majority GeoRIMA `island_region` per connected landmass; other cells take the nearest node's region.

## Feature provenance problems in the national cube (`data/indonesia/processed`)
- **Gravity is Kalimantan-only.** The only satellite gravity file on disk is `data/raw/geophysics/sandwell_smith_gravity_kalimantan.tif` (108.5–119.5°E, −4.5–4.5°N). `load_and_compute_real_gravity_channels` reprojects it onto the national grid and **nearest-fills everywhere else**.
  - Outside Kalimantan, `bouguer_gravity_mgal`, `crustal_thickness_km` and `moho_gravity_step_mgal_km` are artifacts.
  - Bouguer gravity correlates −0.98 with DEM elevation, because the slab correction dominates where the free-air value is a constant fill.
  - All three are excluded here. They stay in the parquet, flagged in `label_provenance.json`.
- **Features used (18):** GEM fault distance, density, slip and dilation tendency; plate-boundary distance; DEM lineaments; volcanic-arc distance and halo; DEM elevation, slope, aspect, local and regional TPI, curvature and TRI; coast distance; regolith and exposure (both DEM-derived).

## Design
- **Grid.** 3.5 km cells, so each point is one cell. 8-connected cells form a deposit cluster: **651 clusters**.
- **Folds and scoring.** Folds are built from 25 km blocks within the scored island, with a 15 km buffer. Deposits are ranked against background cells on the same island. Production XGBoost (100 trees, 5 bags), logistic regression and the single-feature baseline.
- **Conditions per island:**
  - **local:** that island only.
  - **pooled:** that island plus all other islands.
  - **transfer:** other islands only; the scored island is never seen in training.
- **Archipelago-wide.** Plus one archipelago-wide CV run.

## Findings
1. **Adding the whole archipelago does not improve islands that already have data.** For any metal, no island improves significantly with pooling. Kalimantan gets slightly worse (−0.017, p=0.016), and so does Sulawesi (−0.013, not significant). This agrees with the v4.6 result at 250 m.
2. **Pooling helps where an island has almost no examples of a head.**
   - Porphyry in Maluku and Papua, with 5–6 local deposits, goes from near-random to 0.71–0.76.
   - Epithermal gains about 0.03 in three arcs.
   - These are small samples, so treat them as indicative.
3. **Kalimantan and Sulawesi look geologically distinct in these features.** Transfer from other islands loses 0.054 and 0.030 there. Sumatra, Java, Nusa Tenggara, Maluku and Papua transfer almost losslessly, which fits shared volcanic-arc settings.
4. **Laterite pooling hurts.** Ophiolite-hosted laterites in Kalimantan and Sulawesi score worse when trained alongside other islands' laterites. Each region should keep its own laterite model.
5. **The resolution is coarse.** At 3.5 km, Kalimantan any-metal is 0.82 (XGBoost) and 0.69 (logistic regression), against 0.90 / 0.92 at 250 m (v4.6, condition A). These results speak to training extent, not to the production map.

## Caveats
- GeoRIMA nodes are ESDM resource *potential* points of mixed maturity, not verified deposits. Node accuracy and duplication (888 duplicate coordinates across all node types) are not audited.
- The national-cube fault and stress channels follow a third recipe (55 km decay, no lineament blending), different from both the EK and the Kalimantan-wide builds.
- With 3.5 km cells and point labels, adjacent nodes merge into one cluster and sub-cell structure is lost.

## Recommendation
- **Don't pool the archipelago for Kalimantan production models.** Keep per-region training.
- **Use pooled training per head only where local deposits are scarce:** porphyry and epithermal on small or under-sampled islands.
- **Don't build the 1 km archipelago cube just for pooling.** The test does not support it. A 1 km cube would only be worth building to produce maps for other islands; if so, start by fetching archipelago-wide satellite gravity (the national cube's gravity is invalid outside Kalimantan).
